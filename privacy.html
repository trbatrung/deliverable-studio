Privacy

What happens to the details you send.

This site collects only what you type into the enquiry form. Here is what I do with it. Last updated 30 October 2026.

Who is responsible

Deliverable is run by Ethan Truong, working on his own from Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. I am responsible for the personal data described here. You can reach me at hello@deliverable.studio.

Deliverable is based in Vietnam, so Vietnam's personal data protection law applies, and sending the form means you agree to your details being used as described here.

What this site collects

Only what you enter in the enquiry form: your name, your email, your company if you add it, and your message.

The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. Like any website, the hosting provider keeps standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and time of visit, to run and protect the site.

Why I use it

  • To reply to your enquiry. You started the conversation, and replying is what you would expect.
  • If we work together, to prepare a quote and run the project.

Your details are not added to a mailing list, sold, or shared for advertising.

Where it is stored

Form submissions are stored in a database run by Supabase. The site is hosted by Vercel. Both may process data on servers outside your country, and both publish data processing terms that cover international transfers. No one else receives your details unless the law requires it.

How long I keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to a project are deleted within 12 months of our last contact. If we work together, project records are kept for as long as the project needs them and as long as tax and accounting rules require.

Your choices

You can ask me to show you what I hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and you can tell me to stop contacting you. Email hello@deliverable.studio and I will reply within 30 days.

If you are not happy with how I have handled your data, you can complain to the data protection authority where you live, such as the ICO in the UK, the OAIC in Australia, or the PDPC in Singapore.

Changes

If this changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
