Process

From your material to a finished file.

You bring what you already have. I shape the explanation, propose the visuals, and take it through production and review. Two approval points before animation starts, so nothing is a surprise.

I have a deck, some notes and a recording of me explaining it. Is that enough?

That's plenty. I'll come back with a message outline for you to approve.

It starts with what you have

A rough brief, a presentation, a walkthrough, a conversation. You do not need a script, a storyboard or any animation vocabulary.

01

Inputs

Who the video is for, what they should understand or do, where it will be used, and the material you already have.

02

Message and script

I propose the structure and write or refine the script. You approve it before anything is designed.

03

Visual approach

A style frame and a scene plan: what is shown, when, and for how long. You approve it before animation starts.

04

Production

Animation, editing, and audio or captions where agreed. Code-assisted animation and AI tools help with parts of this. I direct and check the result.

05

Review and delivery

You review the piece. Revisions within the agreed scope, then final files in the formats you need.

06

Updates later

When something changes, the update is scoped by what changed. Approved layouts and styles carry forward.

A quote describes the work, not a package.

Before production starts we agree the audience, the message, the approach, the deliverables, the revision scope and the price. For recurring work we agree a scope once the workload is understood.

In every quote
  • Audience and purpose
  • Length, format and where it will be used
  • What you provide and when
  • Approval points: script, visual approach, final review
  • Revision rounds included
  • Delivery formats and timeline

Build the visual approach once. Keep using what works.

A repeatable series does not need a completely new visual language every time. Approved layouts, styles and production decisions can carry forward, while each video gets the explanation it needs.
