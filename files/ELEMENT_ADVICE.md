# Element advice, after the October 2026 UI/UX pass

What the site has now, what to add when it exists, and what to keep out. Ordered by impact.

## Add when it is real
1. **One cleared client piece.** The biggest missing element. When a client allows it, it replaces one concept loop on the homepage (same row pattern, caption "Client project, shared with permission") and gets its own scene list on Examples. Until then every example stays labelled a concept piece.
2. **One short quote from a client or collaborator.** A single line under the relevant row or above the contact form, with name and role. Not a testimonial carousel, not logos without permission.
3. **A starting price or a typical range.** "What does it cost?" currently says quoted on scope. Buyers in AU/UK/SG SMEs usually want a number before they write. If Ethan is comfortable, add one honest line ("Most first projects fall between X and Y") to that FAQ answer and the quote card. Only with a real number.
4. **Real domain and email.** The footer and Contact page still show `hello@deliverable.studio`; on hold by choice. When it lands: canonical and og URLs, sitemap, robots, footer, Contact page.

## Keep as they are
- The conversation + loop rows, one window object, one shadow, one background with dotted dividers, labels only where the subject changes.
- The process video leading the Process section, with the six steps as text below for readers.
- Examples as the one place for full pieces, with clickable scene lists.
- The founder note only on About.

## Keep out
- Stats, "trusted by" strips, invented outcomes, client logos without permission.
- Pricing tiers or packages (the site sells a scoped quote).
- Chat widgets, pop-ups, cookie walls (no tracking is used), autoplaying sound.
- A blog or resources section until there is a steady reason to publish.
- More pages that repeat homepage content.

## Later
- A favicon and mark pass once the domain and brand mark are settled (the favicon is an amber "D").
- Privacy-respecting analytics (Vercel Web Analytics) once outreach brings traffic to learn from.
- Re-render the videos in Geist if the brand moves fully to Geist (they use Inter today).
