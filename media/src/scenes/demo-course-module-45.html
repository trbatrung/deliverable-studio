Giving feedback

Brightline Training, Leadership essentials
Learning objective

By the end of this module, you can give feedback that is specific, fair and easy to act on.

The framework

Build feedback in three steps

1

Observation

Describe what you saw or heard, not what you assume.

2

Impact

Explain the effect it had on the work, the team or you.

3

Request

Ask for one specific change, and agree on it together.

Worked example

Same message, rewritten

Instead of
“You’re always latewith your reports.”
Try
“The last two reports arrived after Friday’s deadline, so the client update slipped. Can we agree on Thursday drafts?”
