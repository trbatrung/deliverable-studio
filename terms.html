Terms

The short version of how this works.

These terms cover this website and the basics of working together. The written quote for your project sets the details, and where the two differ, the quote wins. Last updated 31 October 2026.

This website

The site describes the service in general terms. It is not an offer you can accept. Every example on it is a concept piece made independently for Deliverable. You are welcome to watch them and share links to them, but please do not download, edit or republish them without permission.

Quotes and scope

Each project starts with a written quote. It sets out the audience and purpose, the deliverables, the revision rounds, the timeline and the price. Work starts once you accept the quote, and once any deposit the quote asks for is paid. Changes outside the agreed scope are quoted separately before I do them.

Payment

I invoice in the currency stated on the quote, payable by bank transfer through a service such as Wise or Payoneer. Due dates are on each invoice.

Who owns what

  • Once the invoice is paid in full, you own the final videos and the approved scripts I deliver, and you can use them however you like.
  • Project source files are not included unless the quote says so.
  • My own working methods, and anything I made before or outside your project, stay mine.
  • I will not show your project publicly, including as an example on this site, without your written permission.

Your material

You keep ownership of everything you send me, such as logos, text, figures and recordings, and I use it only for your project. By sending it, you confirm you have the right to use it in the video. I keep anything you mark as confidential to myself.

Ending a project

Either of us can end a project by writing to the other. You pay for the work done up to that point, as set out in the quote, and I hand over whatever is finished.

Monthly plans

Monthly plans are paid in advance. The first month is paid in two parts: a deposit before work starts, and the balance when the first video's preview is ready, before the final files are sent. The deposit is not refunded once work has started. From the second month, each month is invoiced on the 1st and work starts once it is paid. A month already paid is not refunded. You can cancel with 30 days' notice in writing, and the cancellation takes effect at the end of the paid month. Unused credits carry over for one month, then expire.

Liability

I do the work with care and check it before you see it. I am not responsible for indirect losses, such as lost profits. My total liability for a project is limited to the amount you paid for it. None of this limits anything that cannot legally be limited, including your rights under consumer law where you live.

Which law applies

The law named in your quote. If the quote does not name one, the laws of Vietnam.

Questions

Email hello@deliverable.studio. See also the privacy notice.
