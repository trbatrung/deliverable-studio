About

One person doing and directing the work.

Deliverable is run by Ethan Truong, a video producer and editor based in Ho Chi Minh City, working with clients across time zones.

Why I do this work.

I've spent more than seven years producing and editing video, from brand films and testimonials to long-form pieces, and more recently motion graphics built with code. The part I care about most is the moment something complicated becomes clear to the person watching.

Most businesses already know what they need to say. What they lack is the time to shape it and someone to carry it through production. That is the job I take on: the structure, the visual choices, the animation, the checking, and the revisions, until the explanation works.

Direction

Deciding what to show, in what order, for how long. Leading small teams through briefs, revisions and delivery.

Code-assisted motion

Motion graphics built in code, with structured briefs and a repeatable render path, so updates are a script change, not a reshoot.

The tools are not the point. The explanation is.

Code-assisted animation and AI tools help with parts of production. They make considered motion work more practical to commission, repeat and update. They do not decide what a video should say, or whether it works. I do that, and I check the result before you see it.
