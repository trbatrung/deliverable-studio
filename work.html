Status and credits

What these examples are, and are not.

Each piece was written, designed and produced independently for Deliverable, with fictional companies and illustrative content. None is a client result, and none makes a claim about leads, retention or time saved.

What they show is the craft: how a message is structured, what is shown and when, how dense information is held long enough to read, and how a finished file looks. A client project goes through the same process with your material.
