Start a project

What do you need people to understand?

Tell me about your business, who the explanation is for, and where you want to use it. We can work out whether video is the right fit. No animation terminology needed.

What do you need from me to start?
Who the video is for, what they should understand or do, and the material you already have: notes, a presentation, a walkthrough, or a conversation. You do not need a script.
How long does a project take?
It depends on the scope. The timeline is agreed with the quote, with approval points for the message and the visual approach before animation starts.
What does it cost?
A first project is quoted on a defined scope: audience, length, format, deliverables and revision rounds. Ongoing work is agreed once the workload is understood.
Can a video be updated later?
Yes. Updates are scoped by what changes. Approved layouts and styles carry forward, so a revision is usually smaller than the first piece.
Do you need access to our systems or data?
No. For updates and recaps you provide the approved highlights and any figures you want shown. I work from that summary.

Prefer email? hello@deliverable.studio

Contact details and a short description are enough. Please do not include passwords or confidential records.
