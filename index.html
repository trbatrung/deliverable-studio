Practical economics

Build the visual approach once. Keep using what works.

A repeatable series does not need a completely new visual language every time. Approved layouts, styles and production decisions can carry forward, while each video gets the explanation it needs.

Updates and additional pieces are scoped according to what changes. A short diagram-led piece and a bespoke film are different jobs, and the quote reflects that.

Code-assisted animation and AI tools help with parts of production. I direct the work and check the result.

Why I do this work.

I've spent more than seven years producing and editing video, from brand films and testimonials to long-form pieces, and more recently motion graphics built with code. The part I care about most is the moment something complicated becomes clear to the person watching.

Most businesses already know what they need to say. What they lack is the time to shape it and someone to carry it through production. That is the job I take on: the structure, the visual choices, the animation, the checking, and the revisions, until the explanation works.

What do you need people to understand?

Tell me about your business, who the explanation is for, and where you want to use it. We can work out whether video is the right fit.

What do you need from me to start?
Who the video is for, what they should understand or do, and the material you already have: notes, a presentation, a walkthrough, or a conversation. You do not need a script or any animation terminology.
How long does a project take?
It depends on the scope. The timeline is agreed with the quote, with approval points for the message and the visual approach before animation starts.
Can a video be updated later?
Yes. Updates are scoped by what changes. Approved layouts and styles carry forward, so a revision is usually smaller than the first piece.
Do you need access to our systems or data?
No. For updates and recaps you provide the approved highlights and any figures you want shown. I work from that summary.

Contact details and a short description are enough. Please do not include passwords or confidential records.
